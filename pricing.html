Partner pricing

Wholesale, per active employee

from €100 per employee per month

Final rate depends on volume, sectors and how much of the process runs through the API. You set your own price to the client.

Partner fee

Flat monthly fee per active employee. Includes contract, onboarding, monthly payroll, F24 payments, payslips, outputs and ops support.

Employment cost

Gross salary, employer contributions, INAIL, TFR accrual and other statutory costs are passed through monthly on actuals, line by line.

Deposit

One month of employment cost per employee, held as a deposit and returned or offset when employment ends.

Billing

Invoiced monthly in advance, based on the approved payroll register. Payment before the pay date.
